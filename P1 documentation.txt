HTML:
---------------
Hypertext markup language(markup oriented language)
Markup--tag or element
Main purpose is to design the structure and content of the webpage.
Not a programming language.

STRUCTURE OF HTML PAGE:

<Document type>
<html lang=1..>
<html> ----start tag
   <head>
        <title></title> or <script></script> or <meta>
   </head>
   <body bgcolor="red"> content of the webpage</body>--Element
</html> ---end tag

It's not compulsory to have ending tag for each tag. for ex: <or><img><hr>
